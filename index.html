04How to Play

The recipe for victory.

Six ideas, one match. Tap the dice to roll them.

01

Serve it

Open the point with the Serve Die. It sets the first shot your opponent has to return, like Topspin 4↑ or Slice 3↓. Or it calls a LET or a FAULT.

Serve die 
Tap to roll
02

Build the rally

Match the color. Topspin coming in? Play equal or higher. Slice coming in? Play equal or lower. To switch colors with a groundstroke, match the pace (the number is the speed of the ball).

03

Slice it up

Play a Slice card to reverse the momentum. Your opponent must answer with equal or lower pace.

04

Risk it all

Attack the net with a Volley or Drop-Shot and roll for your pace. Just don’t hit it out.

Drop-shot 
Volley 
05

Smash it down

Punish a weak shot with a Smash, defend against an aggressive attack with a Lob, or match their pace with an Approach and steal a card.

06

Highs and lows

Save a Winner for the critical rally, and avoid getting stuck with an Error in the big moments.
